Welcome to L'Enclos

A family bistro run by a father and his son, on rue de la Ville in the heart of old Honfleur, in the former Enclos district that gave it its name. Here, you push open the door as if visiting friends: cobbled lanes, old-world façades and, a few steps away, the Vieux Bassin. At the table, homemade bistro cooking made with local produce, and a wine bar to make the moment last, without rushing.
